# Changelog

## 16001-0.1.8

- Filters moved into a "Filters" window with checkboxes for sources, vendor types and rules. The Upgrades tab keeps the zone box, a Filters button with a count, and Reset, at the top right.
- New rule: "No profession gear" hides gear that needs a profession to wear.

## 16001-0.1.7

- Custom specs: the editor is a pop-up, opened from the spec list with "New spec...". Each custom spec has edit and delete icons in the list.
- Custom specs can be made from the Upgrades tab too. The Simulator shows the slot list at full width.
- The slot rank after the item name shows with any filter on, not only a zone.

## 16001-0.1.6

- Vendor items show what the vendor really asks: currency costs such as Tarnished Undermine Real, items handed in, and the reputation needed. Data from AllTheThings.
- Warsong Gulch rewards show the right faction and standing.
- "limited stock" on items a vendor does not always have.
- "not confirmed" marks vendors whose price or requirements are still being checked.
- The Vendors filter button moved next to Reset.

## 16001-0.1.5

- Vendor items show the vendor type (vendor, reputation, PvP, currency), what the item asks for and the gold price.
- New "Vendors" menu in the filters: hide gold, reputation, PvP rank or other currency vendors.

## 16001-0.1.4

- Character frame: each slot has a button with a crown (best item equipped), an orange arrow (better item known) or a quiet arrow.
- Character frame: upgrades come from every zone, whatever zone is picked in the Upgrades tab.
- Rings and trinkets are each judged against their own item.

## 16001-0.1.3

- Upgrades: with a zone picked, each item shows its rank for the slot after its name.

## 16001-0.1.2

- Zone filter: one search box in place of the Zone button and list. "Anywhere", "Current zone", then all zones by name. Type to search.

## 16001-0.1.1

- The spec is read from your talents: the tree with the most points. Before, a new install started on the first spec of the class.

## 16001-0.1.0

First release.

- Ranked upgrades per slot for your class and spec, with sources.
- Item tooltips: rank, gain, stat changes against the equipped item, best suffix, recommended enchant, source.
- Upgrade arrows on quest rewards, loot and vendors.
- Wanted drops on creature tooltips, map pins, tracker, guide arrow.
- Simulator with custom stat weights.
- Wishlist, loot history, luck stats.
- Roll window with upgrade hints, off by default.
